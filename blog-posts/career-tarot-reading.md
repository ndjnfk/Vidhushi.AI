Work takes up a huge part of our lives, so it's no surprise that career questions are among the most common reasons people come to tarot. Should I change my job? Is a promotion coming? Am I on the right path, or is it time to start something of my own? A career tarot reading can help you step back from the confusion and see your professional situation more clearly.

In this guide, I'll explain how a career tarot reading works, which cards speak most strongly about work and money, a few simple career spreads you can try, and the questions that lead to the most useful answers.

## What is a career tarot reading?

A career tarot reading is a tarot session focused on your work life: your job, your ambitions, your workplace relationships, your finances and the direction you want to grow in. The cards don't hand you a fixed future. They reflect the energies around your situation right now and help you understand what is working, what is blocking you and which choices are open to you.

A career reading can help you:

- Understand what you truly want from your work
- Get clarity before a job change, interview or big decision
- Recognise patterns that keep holding you back
- See the strengths and opportunities you may be overlooking
- Plan your next step with more confidence and less fear

## Key tarot cards for career and money

Any card can appear in a career reading, but some carry an especially strong message about work, success and finances.

### Eight of Pentacles

The card of skill, effort and steady progress. The Eight of Pentacles often appears when you are learning, training or quietly building expertise. It says your hard work is being noticed and will pay off with time.

### Ace of Pentacles

A fresh start in the material world. This card can point to a new job offer, a new source of income or the seed of a business idea. It's an invitation to take a practical first step.

### The Chariot

Drive, focus and victory. The Chariot appears when you need to take control of your direction and push forward with determination. In career readings, it often signals success that comes from discipline.

### Three of Pentacles

Teamwork and recognition. This card highlights collaboration, good colleagues and work that is valued by others. It can also suggest that the right partnership will move your goals forward.

### Ten of Pentacles

Long-term stability and lasting wealth. The Ten of Pentacles speaks of security, a solid foundation and building something that lasts, whether that's a career, a family business or financial safety.

### Five of Pentacles

Worry, loss or feeling left out in the cold. It isn't an easy card, but in a career reading it often asks you to reach out for support and to remember that difficult phases pass.

### The Tower

Sudden change. The Tower can show an unexpected shake-up such as a restructure, a job loss or a big realisation. As hard as it feels, it often clears the way for a path that suits you better.

Remember: no single card tells the full story. Its meaning depends on its position in the spread, the cards around it and your exact question. That's why a personal reading gives far more insight than looking up one card online.

## Popular career tarot spreads

### 1. The three-card career spread

A quick, clear spread for almost any work question:

- **Where I am now:** my current career situation
- **What is blocking me:** the main challenge or obstacle
- **Next step:** the advice for moving forward

### 2. The job-change spread

Useful when you are deciding whether to stay or go:

- **If I stay:** what staying in my current job brings
- **If I leave:** what a change would bring
- **What I need to know:** the hidden factor in this decision

### 3. The "right path" spread

For when you feel stuck or unsure what you are meant to do:

- What I'm naturally good at
- What fulfils me
- What is stopping me from following it
- How I can start moving towards it

## Best questions to ask in a career tarot reading

The quality of a reading depends a lot on the question. Open questions usually bring clearer guidance than simple yes-or-no ones.

Instead of: *"Will I get this job?"*
Try: *"What do I need to know to give myself the best chance with this opportunity?"*

Instead of: *"Will I be rich?"*
Try: *"What can I change to build more financial stability?"*

A few more helpful questions:

- What is the energy around my career right now?
- Is this the right time to ask for a promotion or raise?
- What skills should I focus on developing?
- What is holding me back from growing at work?
- Should I explore starting my own business?

## Can tarot predict a job or promotion?

Tarot shows the energies and the likely direction of your situation as it stands today. Because you have free will, your choices and actions can change the outcome. A good career reading won't simply say "yes" or "no". It will help you understand your position, recognise your options and decide how to move forward.

Tarot is a tool for reflection and spiritual guidance. It should not replace professional financial, legal or career advice.

## Why a personal career tarot reading helps

Card meanings online are a good starting point, but they can't consider your story: your field, your goals, your fears and the choice in front of you. In a personal reading, the cards are read together around *your* question, so the guidance is specific and practical.
